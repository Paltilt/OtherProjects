# Ephemeral Peace of Mind

*A narrative-driven 3D experience about inner turmoil, resilience, and the experience of feeling lost.*

**Ephemeral Peace of Mind** was a collaborative master's project I took part in during my time at the University of applied sciences Salzburg. The project was focused on helping people empathise with people struggling with depression. It is an interactive experience which follows a character navigating daily life with severe depressions and explores a dark parallel world that reflects their emotional struggles. Through exploration, narrative choices, and atmospheric visuals, users gradually uncover the truth behind the character's inner turmoil and help them overcome it.

## Project Highlights

- Narrative-focused interactions centered on emotional challenges and mental well-being.
- Exploration of a dark parallel world with immersive 3D visuals and audio.
- Player decisions that shape the character's journey.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration across programming, game design, art, writing, and audio.

## My Contribution

My role in the project mainly revolved around game programming and interdisziplinary communication. I was mainly responsible for conceptualising and implementing the backend logic for the psychological state of the character and connecting it to the interactions. I was also responsible for the communication between the programming and art team helping in delegating workloads to the right people and providing the right programming logic for assets.
