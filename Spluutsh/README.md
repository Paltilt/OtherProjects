*A fast-paced first-person 3D platformer FPS built around fluid movement, creative traversal, and speedrunning in a futuristic cyberpunk world.*

**Spluutsh** is a movement-focused jump'n'run FPS game, which I developed back in 2023 at the University of applied sciences Salzburg. In this game players race against the clock, combining running, sliding, and jumping to navigate a challenging level as efficiently as possible. Its central mechanic allows players to coat surfaces with two types of colorful "Spluutsh," each changing how the character moves. One provides a speed boost, while the other launches the player into powerful jumps. Mastering the interaction between these abilities, preserving momentum, and discovering alternate routes are key to achieving a smooth, fast run.

## Project Highlights

- Fast-paced first-person platforming with a focus on momentum, flow, and speedrunning.
- Two surface-based Spluutsh abilities that enhance speed or jumping.
- Movement combinations involving running, sliding, jumping, and carefully timed ability usage.
- A futuristic cyberpunk setting with opportunities to discover alternate paths.

## My Contribution

The project was made by a small team of three people, with two artists providing some of the assets. As a programmer, my main focus was on implementing the movement. This included features like wall-running and the "Spluutsh".
